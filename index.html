Consignment tracker

Check where a Shree Tirupati Courier shipment is. Enter the 12-digit AWB number from the booking receipt, or several numbers separated by commas.

Up to 25 numbers at a time.

How to use

  1. 1

    Enter the AWB number

    Use the 12-digit number on the booking receipt. To track several at once, separate them with commas (up to 25).

  2. 2

    Press Track

    Status, route and tracking history come live from the courier’s public tracking.

  3. 3

    Refresh or verify

    Use Refresh for the latest update, or Open on courier site to see the official page.

Follow your shipment from booking to delivery. Enter the 12-digit AWB number from your booking receipt to see its route, current status and full tracking history.
