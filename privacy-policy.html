Privacy Policy

How AWB Tracker handles your information, in plain language.

Last updated: 22 November 2026

In short: you don’t need an account to use AWB Tracker. The AWB numbers you search are passed to the courier’s tracking service to get your result, and we don’t keep a database of them. Your recent searches stay in your own browser. We don’t sell your information, and we don’t use advertising or analytics cookies.

Who we are

AWB Tracker (“we”, “us”, “our”) is run by [Business Name]. This policy explains what happens to information when you use this website, which lets you check the status of Shree Tirupati Courier shipments. If you have any questions, you can reach us using the details at the end of this page.

Information we handle

AWB numbers you search for

When you press Track, your browser sends the AWB number or numbers to our server, which passes them to the courier’s public tracking service and returns the result to you. We don’t store AWB numbers or results in a database of our own.

Tracking results

The courier’s tracking can include a consignee’s name, the places a parcel travelled between and key dates. We show this to you and don’t keep it.

Recent searches

To save you retyping, your browser remembers your last few searches. This list is kept in your browser’s local storage, on your own device, and is never sent to us.

Messages you send us

If you use the contact form, we receive your name, your email address, the topic, your message, an AWB number if you choose to add one, and your confirmation that you agree to this policy. We use these only to reply to you and to keep a record of the conversation.

Technical information

Like any website, our hosting provider receives basic technical details whenever a page or file is requested: your IP address, browser and device type, the address requested and the time. These are used to deliver the site, keep it secure and fix faults.

How we use it

We use information only to:

  • show you tracking results;
  • reply to your messages and requests;
  • keep the website secure and prevent misuse;
  • find and fix problems and keep the service working reliably;
  • meet our legal obligations.

We don’t sell your information, and we don’t use it for advertising or profiling. Where the law requires a reason for using personal information, ours are your consent (for example, when you send us a message), our legitimate interest in running and protecting the service, and legal requirements.

Cookies and browser storage

AWB Tracker doesn’t set cookies of its own, and we don’t use analytics, advertising or social-media trackers. The one thing we store in your browser is your list of recent searches (see above), which you can delete at any time by clearing this site’s data in your browser settings.

The pages load their fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google’s servers to download them, which shares your IP address and browser details with Google. If you follow a link to the courier’s website, it may use cookies under its own policy.

If we ever add analytics, advertising or other third-party services, we will update this policy first and, where the law requires, ask for your consent.

Who we share it with

We share information only with the services that make AWB Tracker work:

  • The courier’s tracking service receives the AWB numbers you search, because that is how a result is found. It is run by the courier under its own policies.
  • Netlify, our hosting provider, serves this website, runs the small program that fetches your results and collects contact-form messages for us.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces used on the site, as described above.

We may also disclose information if the law, a court or a public authority requires it, or to protect our rights, our users or the security of the service. We don’t sell personal information.

How long we keep it

  • Searches and results: we don’t keep them.
  • Recent searches: they stay in your browser until you clear them.
  • Contact messages: we keep them only as long as needed to reply and to keep a record of the conversation, and then we delete them.
  • Technical logs: our hosting provider keeps these for a limited period under its own schedule.

You can ask us to delete a message you sent at any time (see “Your choices and rights”).

Where it is processed

Our hosting provider and Google run services around the world, so information about your visit may be processed in countries other than the one you live in, including outside India. These providers are responsible for protecting it under their own terms and the law that applies to them.

Keeping it safe

This website is served over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection, and we collect only the personal information we need. No system is completely secure, though, so we can’t guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, data-protection laws may give you rights over your personal information, such as the right to:

  • ask what information we hold about you and get a copy;
  • have inaccurate information corrected;
  • have your information deleted;
  • withdraw a consent you gave us earlier;
  • make a complaint.

The only personal information we are likely to hold is a message you sent through the contact form. To use any of these rights, email [Support Email] from the address you used. We may need to confirm it is you before acting, and we will respond within the time the law requires. You can also clear your recent searches at any time in your browser.

Children

AWB Tracker isn’t aimed at children, and we don’t knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you think a child has sent us their details, please contact us and we will delete them.

Links to other websites

This website links to the courier’s website and may link to others. We don’t control them and aren’t responsible for how they handle information, so please read their privacy policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our practices or the law change. The “Last updated” date at the top shows the latest version. If a change is significant, we will make it clear on this page.
