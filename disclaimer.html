Information only

The information on AWB Tracker is provided for general information and convenience. It isn’t a promise, guarantee or confirmation of when a parcel will be delivered, where it is at this moment or what condition it is in.

Where the information comes from

When you search, AWB Tracker asks Shree Tirupati Courier’s public tracking service about your AWB number and shows you what it returns. We don’t create, edit or check that data. A result is only as accurate and up to date as the courier’s own records at the moment you look.

Delays, gaps and errors

Tracking updates depend on parcels being scanned and on the courier’s staff recording them. You may therefore see:

  • a status that is behind where the parcel really is;
  • “Status not updated”, or an empty history, even though the parcel is moving;
  • “Not found” for a number that was only just booked, or that was typed incorrectly;
  • wording or details that look odd, because they are shown exactly as the courier recorded them.

AWB Tracker may also show incomplete or wrong results if the courier changes its website or has a fault in its systems. If something doesn’t look right, press Refresh, check again later, or use Open on courier site to see the official page.

We are not the courier

AWB Tracker isn’t the courier and doesn’t collect, carry or deliver parcels. We can’t change a delivery, trace a missing parcel, arrange a redelivery or settle a claim for delay, loss or damage. For any of these, contact the courier directly using the help desk number shown with your tracking result, or through shreetirupaticourier.net.

Availability of the service

AWB Tracker depends on the courier’s website being available. It may be slow, show errors or be unavailable at times, and we may change or pause it without notice. We don’t promise that it will always be available or free of errors.

Personal details in results

Tracking results can include the consignee’s name, places and dates. The address in your browser after a search also contains the AWB number, so anyone who opens that link can run the same search. Share links and screenshots only with people who should see that information.
