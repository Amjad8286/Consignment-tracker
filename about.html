About AWB Tracker

A simple, free way to see where your Shree Tirupati Courier shipment is. No sign-up and no app to install.

What AWB Tracker does

Every Shree Tirupati Courier booking comes with a 12-digit AWB (air waybill) number, printed on your receipt. Type it in and AWB Tracker shows you the route your parcel is on, its latest status, the booking details and the full tracking history, laid out clearly on a phone, tablet or computer.

Following more than one parcel? Enter up to 25 numbers, separated by commas, and check them all in one go.

  • Fast and simple

    Enter a number and press Track. There is nothing to sign up for or install.

  • Many parcels at once

    Check up to 25 AWB numbers in a single search.

  • Clear route and status

    See where a parcel started, where it is going and what the courier last reported.

  • Made for every screen

    A clean, readable layout that is comfortable on phones, tablets and desktops.

How it works

  1. 1

    You enter an AWB number

    Type the 12-digit number from your booking receipt, or paste several.

  2. 2

    We ask the courier

    AWB Tracker makes the same public search the courier’s own website offers and reads the answer.

  3. 3

    You see the result

    Route, status, booking details and history appear in a clean layout. Nothing in the courier’s system is changed.

Every detail you see comes from the courier’s tracking system. AWB Tracker doesn’t make it up, edit it or add to it, so a result is only as current as the courier’s latest scan. If something looks out of date, press Refresh or check again a little later.

What we stand for

  • Clarity. Status, route and dates up front, without clutter or jargon.
  • Honesty. We show what the courier reports. When there is nothing to show, we say so rather than guess.
  • Privacy. No accounts, and we don’t sell your information. Your recent searches stay in your own browser. Read our Privacy Policy.
